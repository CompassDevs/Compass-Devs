About Compass Devs

Independent. Curious. Always Building.

Compass Devs is an independent development studio creating games and apps for curious minds. From relaxing puzzles to practical tools, the goal is simple: build digital experiences that add a little more adventure, knowledge and enjoyment to everyday life.

Compass Devs began as a three-person idea around the first version of Rombicapo. The original iOS game reached working phones but never reached the App Store. Years later, the project was revived independently, rebuilt with Godot and eventually brought to both Android and iOS.

That release became the spark for more experiments: from 3D prototypes such as Road Roller and Jumpzy, to Cute Birds and practical marine tools born from engineering work. Over time the catalogue also expanded into other ideas, including tide utilities and AI-powered tools.

Today Compass Devs is an independent one-person studio, developed alongside a full-time engineering career and family life. Some projects are current, others belong to earlier generations of the portfolio, but together they document a continuing process of learning, experimenting and building.

The common thread has stayed the same: learn by building, solve a real need when one appears, and turn ideas worth exploring into working software. The technology and visual style continue to evolve, while the Compass Devs identity remains centered on exploration, practical creativity and independent development.

TideLive World

Live tide-gauge information and tidal predictions delivered from a backend to mobile devices.

About TideLive

TideLive grew from practical marine and engineering needs. The system was designed around a backend that manages tide-gauge information and a mobile frontend that makes live and predicted tidal information easier to view in the field.

The tutorials on this page show both sides of that workflow: the frontend used to access tide information and the backend used to manage the data behind it.

Marine • Forecast

OmniTides

Worldwide predicted tides with fast station discovery and practical tide information.

About OmniTides

OmniTides brings worldwide predicted tide information into a mobile interface designed for quick station discovery and everyday use. The app lets users begin by adding a station, search nearby stations using GPS, or browse stations manually by continent and list.

The project carries forward the marine-software side of Compass Devs: taking experience gained from practical engineering work and turning it into a more approachable mobile tool for finding and exploring tide stations around the world.

OmniTides Pro

The expanded OmniTides experience with additional station-discovery tools and a broader tide, weather and lunar toolkit.

About OmniTides Pro

OmniTides Pro expands the OmniTides concept for users who want more ways to discover tide stations and explore environmental information. Stations can be selected through GPS, nearby-station maps, lists or random discovery.

About ChatZap

ChatZap was a Compass Devs exploration of conversational AI in a friendly mobile format. The app combined general chat with guided questions, learning-oriented prompts, image generation, conversation history and multilingual support.

Its design aimed to make different AI capabilities easy to discover from a single home screen, with light and dark themes and support for ten languages. The original promotional material below documents that release.
