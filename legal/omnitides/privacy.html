OmniTides Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Compass Devs ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use OmniTides.

1. Information Collected by Compass Devs

OmniTides does not require you to create a Compass Devs account.

Compass Devs does not sell, rent, or use your personal information for advertising purposes through OmniTides.

OmniTides is provided without third-party advertising in the version covered by this Privacy Policy.

App settings, selected stations, preferences and similar information may be stored locally on your device. Information stored only on your device is not transmitted to Compass Devs.

2. Location

OmniTides may request access to your device location when you choose to use features such as finding nearby tide stations.

Location access is used to provide the feature you request. Compass Devs does not use OmniTides to create a location-history profile or for advertising.

You can deny or revoke location permission at any time through your device settings. Features that depend on location may then be unavailable or may require manual station selection.

3. Internet and Tide Information

OmniTides may use an internet connection to obtain tide, station, weather, map or other related information needed by supported features.

Normal internet communications may provide technical information, such as an IP address and network request information, to the services used to deliver requested data. Such third-party services are governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

4. Advertising and Tracking

OmniTides contains no third-party advertising in the version covered by this Privacy Policy.

Compass Devs does not use OmniTides to track users across apps or websites for advertising purposes and does not sell personal information.

5. Analytics

Compass Devs does not use OmniTides to build advertising profiles. If a future version introduces analytics or another third-party service that changes how personal information is handled, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

6. Children's Privacy

OmniTides is a general-purpose tide-information utility and does not require a Compass Devs account.

Compass Devs does not knowingly request or directly collect personal information from children through a Compass Devs account or server.

7. Data Security

Compass Devs takes reasonable measures appropriate to the information handled by OmniTides. No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Your Choices

You can control location permission and other device permissions through your operating-system settings.

Locally stored OmniTides information can generally be removed by clearing the application's local data or uninstalling the app, subject to your operating system's backup and restore functionality.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if OmniTides functionality, third-party services, or applicable requirements change. The date displayed at the top of this page indicates when this Privacy Policy was last updated.
