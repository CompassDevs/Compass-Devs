Rombicapo Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Compass Devs ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Rombicapo on Android or iOS.

1. Information Collected by Compass Devs

Rombicapo does not require you to create a Compass Devs account.

Compass Devs does not operate servers for Rombicapo and does not directly collect, store, sell, rent, or share your personal information through the game.

Rombicapo does not contain third-party advertising and does not use third-party analytics or crash-reporting services.

Game settings, preferences, statistics, progress and other game information may be stored locally on your device. Information stored only on your device is not transmitted to Compass Devs.

2. Google Play Games

On supported Android devices, Rombicapo may use Google Play Games services for features such as player authentication, achievements and leaderboards.

When you use these features, Google may process information associated with your Google Play Games profile and gameplay, including player identity, scores, achievements and certain device or network information.

Google Play Games is operated by Google. Information processed by Google is subject to Google's own privacy policies and account settings. Compass Devs does not operate the Google Play Games service.

3. Apple Game Center

On supported Apple devices, Rombicapo may use Apple Game Center for features such as player authentication, achievements and leaderboards.

Game Center is operated by Apple. Information associated with your Game Center account and your use of Game Center is handled by Apple according to Apple's privacy policies and your Apple account settings.

Rombicapo may interact with Game Center to submit or retrieve information necessary for supported features, such as scores, achievements, leaderboard information and player identifiers.

4. Multiplayer

Rombicapo supports multiplayer gameplay between compatible devices using a local network or Wi-Fi connection.

Devices participating in a multiplayer game may communicate directly with each other as necessary to establish and conduct the game.

Compass Devs does not operate a Rombicapo multiplayer server and does not receive or store information exchanged solely between devices during local multiplayer gameplay.

5. Advertising and Tracking

Rombicapo contains no third-party advertising.

Compass Devs does not use Rombicapo to track users across apps or websites for advertising purposes and does not sell personal information.

6. Analytics

Rombicapo does not use Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, or another third-party analytics service operated by Compass Devs.

7. Children's Privacy

Rombicapo does not knowingly request or directly collect personal information from children through a Compass Devs account or server.

Platform services such as Google Play Games and Apple Game Center are governed by the respective provider's policies, account requirements, parental controls and privacy settings.

8. Data Security

Because Compass Devs does not operate Rombicapo user accounts or game-data servers, Compass Devs does not maintain a central database containing Rombicapo players' personal information.

Information processed by Google Play Games or Apple Game Center is subject to the security and privacy practices of those respective providers.

9. Your Choices

You can control Google Play Games and Apple Game Center through the privacy, account and gaming settings provided by Google and Apple.

Locally stored Rombicapo information can generally be removed by clearing the application's local data or uninstalling the game, subject to your operating system's backup and restore functionality.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if Rombicapo's functionality, third-party services, or applicable requirements change. The date displayed at the top of this page indicates when this Privacy Policy was last updated.
