Wacky Jump Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Compass Devs ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Wacky Jump.

1. Information Collected by Compass Devs

Wacky Jump does not require you to create a Compass Devs account.

Compass Devs does not operate a Wacky Jump user-account server and does not directly collect, sell, rent, or share your personal information through normal gameplay.

Wacky Jump does not contain third-party advertising in the version covered by this Privacy Policy.

Game settings, preferences, progress and other game information may be stored locally on your device. Information stored only on your device is not transmitted to Compass Devs.

2. Advertising and Tracking

Wacky Jump contains no third-party advertising in the version covered by this Privacy Policy.

Compass Devs does not use Wacky Jump to track users across apps or websites for advertising purposes and does not sell personal information.

3. Analytics

Wacky Jump does not require a Compass Devs analytics account or player profile. If a future version introduces analytics, crash reporting, online services or another third-party service that changes how information is handled, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

4. Children's Privacy

Wacky Jump does not require a Compass Devs account and does not knowingly request or directly collect personal information from children through a Compass Devs server.

5. Data Security

Because Compass Devs does not operate Wacky Jump user accounts or a central gameplay-data server for the version covered by this policy, Compass Devs does not maintain a central database of Wacky Jump player profiles.

6. Your Choices

Locally stored Wacky Jump information can generally be removed by clearing the application's local data or uninstalling the game, subject to your operating system's backup and restore functionality.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if Wacky Jump functionality, third-party services, or applicable requirements change. The date displayed at the top of this page indicates when this Privacy Policy was last updated.
