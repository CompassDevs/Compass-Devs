Privacy Policy

How Compass Devs handles information in the Wacky Jump prototype.

Last updated: 26 October 2026
Read Terms & ConditionsBack to Compass Devs
Prototype status. Wacky Jump is currently a Compass Devs prototype. This policy reflects the prototype’s present privacy design and should be reviewed before any store release that adds online or third-party services.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Compass Devs is the developer responsible for the processing described in this policy. Contact: compassdevs@gmail.com.

2. Data used by the prototype

  • Local gameplay information. Settings, preferences or progress may be stored locally on your device where supported.
  • Support communications. If you contact Compass Devs, we receive your email address and any information you choose to provide.

3. Data not required by the current prototype

The current Wacky Jump prototype does not require a Compass Devs account and is not designed to collect precise location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings or user-generated social content. It is not designed to use personal data for targeted advertising.

4. Analytics, advertising and third-party services

The prototype covered by this policy is not intended to rely on behavioural advertising or user-tracking services. If a later release adds analytics, crash reporting, advertising, online leaderboards, cloud saves or other third-party SDKs, this policy and the applicable store disclosures will be updated before or with that release.

5. Retention

Local game data remains on the device until it is changed, cleared or the app is uninstalled. Support emails may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, keep records, prevent abuse or satisfy legal obligations.

6. Children

Wacky Jump does not require a Compass Devs account and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. A parent or guardian who believes personal information has been provided to Compass Devs may contact us.

7. Your rights

Where applicable privacy law gives you rights over personal data held directly by Compass Devs, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or portability as applicable. Contact us using the email above. You may also lodge a complaint with the competent data-protection authority.

8. Changes to this policy

Wacky Jump remains in development. This policy will be updated if the game becomes a public release or starts using additional personal data or third-party services.
