Tile Square — Privacy Policy

Effective: 3 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tile Square, developed by Compass Devs, handles information when you use the game.

Privacy approach

Tile Square is designed as an ad-free game. Compass Devs does not include advertising SDKs to serve ads and does not require a separate Compass Devs or Tile Square account.

Leaderboards and platform gaming services

Tile Square supports leaderboards through platform gaming services such as Google Play Games Services on Android and Apple Game Center on Apple platforms, where available. When you use these services, the platform provider may process information needed for authentication and leaderboards, which may include a player identifier, nickname/display name, avatar, scores, achievements or other game activity, and technical or diagnostic information.

Leaderboard scores and associated player information may be visible to other users according to your platform privacy settings. Google and Apple control their respective account, retention, privacy and deletion mechanisms.

Analytics and crash reporting

Compass Devs does not intentionally integrate a separate third-party analytics or crash-reporting service into Tile Square. Platform services used by the game may independently collect diagnostics, analytics or service data under their own privacy policies.

Local game data

Tile Square may store settings, preferences, progress, records and similar gameplay information locally on your device so the game can remember your choices and progress. Local data generally remains on the device unless a platform feature you use provides synchronization or storage.

Advertising and sale of data

Tile Square contains no advertising. Compass Devs does not sell personal information collected through Tile Square and does not use Tile Square data for targeted advertising.

Third-party services

Google Play Games Services and Apple Game Center are provided and controlled by Google and Apple. Their processing of information is governed by their own terms and privacy policies. Relevant gaming-profile and privacy settings can be managed through your Google or Apple account and device settings.

Children

Tile Square does not knowingly ask users to provide personal information directly to Compass Devs through the game. Platform gaming services may apply age-dependent account, privacy and sharing rules. Parents or guardians should review the applicable platform family and privacy settings.

Retention and deletion

Compass Devs does not operate a separate Tile Square user-account system. Local game data can generally be removed by clearing app data or uninstalling the game, subject to device and platform behavior. Leaderboard/profile information handled by Google Play Games Services or Apple Game Center is managed by those providers and their deletion controls.

Security

We aim to minimize information handled directly by Compass Devs. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure, and third-party platform services are subject to their own security practices.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy if Tile Square's features, services or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when a revised policy is published.
