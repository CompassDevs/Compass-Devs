Road Roller Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Compass Devs ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Road Roller.

1. Information Collected by Compass Devs

Road Roller does not require you to create a Compass Devs account.

Compass Devs does not operate a Road Roller user-account server and does not directly collect, sell, rent, or share your personal information through normal gameplay.

Road Roller does not contain third-party advertising in the version covered by this Privacy Policy.

Game settings, preferences and progress may be stored locally on your device. Information stored only on your device is not transmitted to Compass Devs.

2. Motion Sensors

Road Roller may use motion-sensor information from your device, such as accelerometer or gyroscope data, to control gameplay.

This sensor information is used for game controls and is not intended to identify you or create a profile about you.

Compass Devs does not use motion-sensor information for advertising purposes.

3. Advertising and Tracking

Road Roller contains no third-party advertising in the version covered by this Privacy Policy.

Compass Devs does not use Road Roller to track users across apps or websites for advertising purposes and does not sell personal information.

4. Analytics

Road Roller does not require a Compass Devs analytics account or user profile. If a future version introduces analytics, crash reporting or another third-party service that changes how information is handled, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

5. Children's Privacy

Road Roller does not require a Compass Devs account and does not knowingly request or directly collect personal information from children through a Compass Devs server.

6. Data Security

Because Compass Devs does not operate Road Roller user accounts or a central gameplay-data server for the version covered by this policy, Compass Devs does not maintain a central database of Road Roller player profiles.

7. Your Choices

Locally stored Road Roller information can generally be removed by clearing the application's local data or uninstalling the game, subject to your operating system's backup and restore functionality.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if Road Roller functionality, third-party services, or applicable requirements change. The date displayed at the top of this page indicates when this Privacy Policy was last updated.
